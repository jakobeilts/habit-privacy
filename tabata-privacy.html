Privacy Policy

Tabata App — last updated 25 April 2026

Tabata App ("the app") is an iOS interval-timer application developed by Jakob Eilts ("the developer"). This document explains what data the app handles and how it is stored. The app is designed to collect as little information as possible.

Summary

  • The app does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.
  • The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • The app does not require an account and does not ask for your email, name, or any other identifier.
  • Workouts you create are stored on your device and, optionally, in your own private iCloud account.

What the app stores

The app stores the workouts you create — their name, icon, work and rest durations, number of rounds, and any custom labels. This data lives in two places:

  • On your device. Workouts are saved to a JSON file inside the app's sandboxed Application Support directory. Only the app can read this file. Deleting the app permanently removes it.
  • In your private iCloud database (optional). If you are signed in to iCloud and CloudKit is enabled in the app, workouts are also synced to your private CloudKit container. Apple manages this storage and applies its standard iCloud privacy and security protections. The developer cannot access this data; it is tied to your Apple ID. See Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how Apple handles iCloud data.

What the app does not do

  • It does not contact any server controlled by the developer.
  • It does not include analytics, crash reporting, or advertising frameworks.
  • It does not request access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or health data.
  • It does not read or modify any files outside its own sandbox.

Audio

The app plays short beeps during workouts. It uses the AVAudioSession "playback / mix with others" mode, which means it will play alongside any music or podcast you are already listening to without stopping or ducking that audio. No microphone access is requested.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Because no personal data is collected at all, no age-based data handling is required.

Your choices

  • To stop using iCloud sync, sign out of iCloud or disable iCloud Drive for the app in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
  • To remove all workouts, delete the app. This removes the local cache. Workouts in your private iCloud database can be removed via Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the updated version will be posted at the same URL where you are reading this document, with a new "last updated" date.
