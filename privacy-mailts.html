Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 20, 2026

Mailts ("we", "the app") is a household organization app built by an independent developer. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data Mailts collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

1. Data We Collect

Mailts collects and stores the following data, which you provide directly through using the app:

  • Account information: Your display name within the household.
  • Household data: Household name, invite code, and membership information.
  • Task and reward data: Task titles, descriptions, point values, icons, and completion status.
  • Activity feed: Records of completed tasks and redeemed rewards, including the member name and timestamp.

Mailts does not collect your real name, email address, phone number, location, or any device identifiers beyond what iCloud uses for authentication.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored exclusively in Apple's CloudKit (iCloud) infrastructure. Mailts does not use any third-party servers, databases, analytics services, or tracking tools. Your data resides in Apple's data centers and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

A small amount of data (your household and member identifiers) is stored locally on your device using UserDefaults to keep you signed in between sessions.

3. How Your Data Is Used

Your data is used solely to provide the app's functionality:

  • Displaying tasks, rewards, and feed items to household members.
  • Tracking points earned and spent.
  • Sending push notifications about household activity (if you opt in).

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party. We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

4. Data Sharing Within Your Household

Mailts is a shared household app. All members of the same household can see:

  • All tasks and rewards in the household.
  • The activity feed (who completed which task, who redeemed which reward).
  • Each member's name and point balance.
  • Tasks assigned to other members.

This shared visibility is a core feature of the app. Only people who have your household's invite code can join and see this data.

5. Push Notifications

Mailts offers optional push notifications for household activity. You can choose to receive notifications for task completions, reward redemptions, both, or neither. Notification preferences can be changed at any time in the app's Household settings. Push notifications are delivered through Apple's Push Notification Service (APNs).

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains in CloudKit as long as the household exists. If you leave a household, your membership data remains visible to other household members (your name and past activity in the feed). To request complete deletion of your data, please contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

Mailts does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The app requires an iCloud account, which is subject to Apple's age requirements. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data the app holds about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Leave a household at any time, removing your active participation.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to exercise your data rights, or need the developer's postal address, please contact us at: jakobeilts@web.de
