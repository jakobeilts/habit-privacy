Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Go to Settings (gear icon) and tap "Restore Purchases". This will restore any previous Pro purchases linked to your Apple ID.

How do shared habits work?

Create a habit and enable "Share with a friend". You'll get an invite code to share. Your friend opens the app, taps the + menu, selects "Join Shared Habit", and enters the code. Once paired, you can both track the same habit and see each other's progress.

Why aren't my shared habits syncing?

Shared habits require an active iCloud account. Make sure you're signed into iCloud on your device (Settings > Apple ID > iCloud). Also check your internet connection. You can pull down on the Habits tab to force a refresh.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Go to your iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions, find "Habit and Mood Tracking", and tap "Cancel Subscription".

Can I recover deleted habits or trackers?

Yes! Deleted items are kept in the Trash for 30 days. Go to Settings > Trash to restore them. After 30 days, they are permanently removed.

How do widgets work?

Long-press your home screen, tap the + button, and search for "Habit and Mood Tracking". You can add home screen widgets to see your habits at a glance, or lock screen widgets for quick progress checks. Some widgets are interactive — you can complete habits directly from the widget.

What data do you collect?

We don't use any analytics or tracking tools. Your data stays on your device. Shared habits sync through Apple's iCloud. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
