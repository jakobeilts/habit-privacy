Why Mailts?

🏠

Shared Household

Create a household and invite everyone with a simple 6-character code. Everyone sees the same tasks and rewards in real time.

✅

Flexible Tasks

Recurring chores, one-time to-dos, and personal tasks. Each type works differently so nothing falls through the cracks.

⭐

Points & Rewards

Earn points for every completed task. Spend them on rewards your household defines — movie night, sleeping in, you name it.

📣

Live Feed

See what everyone's been up to. Completed a task? Redeemed a reward? It shows up in the feed instantly.

🔔

Push Notifications

Get notified when someone completes a task or redeems a reward. Choose exactly what you want to hear about.

🔒

Private & Secure

Built on Apple's CloudKit — your data stays in iCloud. No third-party servers, no tracking, no ads.

How It Works

1

Create or join a household

One person creates the household and shares the invite code. Everyone else joins with that code.

2

Add tasks and rewards

Define recurring chores, one-time tasks, or personal to-dos. Set point values and pick an icon.

3

Complete tasks, earn points

Check off tasks from the backlog or your personal list. Points are added to your balance automatically.

4

Redeem rewards

Saved up enough? Spend your points on the rewards your household has agreed on.
